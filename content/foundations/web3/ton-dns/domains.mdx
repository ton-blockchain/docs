Names directly under `.ton` follow the registration, auction, and renewal rules of the official domain collection. These rules apply independently of the application used to manage the domain. Use [dns.ton.org](/foundations/web3/ton-dns/manage-domains) for registration and management through the website.

## Registration rules

The label before `.ton` must contain 4 to 126 characters: lowercase ASCII letters (`a-z`), digits (`0-9`), or hyphens. A hyphen cannot be the first or last character. For example, `example.ton` has the label `example`.

These restrictions apply to registration in the `.ton` collection. The broader name format in [TEP-81](/foundations/web3/ton-dns/reference#name-encoding) also supports names served by other resolvers. Delegated subdomains follow their resolver's rules.

## Minimum first bids

The minimum opening bid depends on the label's length, excluding `.ton`:

| Characters | Minimum first bid (GRAM) |
| ---------: | -----------------------: |
|          4 |                      100 |
|          5 |                       50 |
|          6 |                       40 |
|          7 |                       30 |
|          8 |                       20 |
|          9 |                       10 |
|         10 |                        5 |
|     11–126 |                        1 |

Transaction fees are additional. The final price depends on competing bids.

## Auctions

Registration starts an auction for an unregistered name. New-domain auctions initially last one hour. Each competing bid must be at least 5% higher than the previous bid, with integer rounding in nanograms. A bid in the final hour extends the deadline to one hour after that bid.

The collection deploys the domain contract and forwards the remaining message value after fees. The accepted first bid can therefore differ from the submitted value.

After the deadline, the highest bidder can take ownership. Ownership is recorded when a further successful transaction finalizes the auction. The website displays the winner before that finalization; an owner record update can finalize it. See the [finalization interface](/foundations/web3/ton-dns/reference#auction-finalization) for contract behavior.

## Ownership

Each domain is a transferable [non-fungible token (NFT)](/foundations/glossary#non-fungible-token-nft). Its owner controls the DNS records and can transfer the domain to another account.

Ownership and payment routing are separate: the `wallet` record can point to an account other than the owner. Transferring ownership keeps the existing DNS records, so a new owner must review payment and delegation destinations.

## Renewal

A domain's renewal period is 366 days from its last qualifying transaction. A successful owner record update, content replacement, ownership transfer, or balance top-up resets that period. An empty-body top-up qualifies after auction finalization. The [renewal interface](/foundations/web3/ton-dns/reference#renewal-interface) specifies the eligible messages.

Renewal starts a new period from execution time. It does not add 366 days to the previous deadline. A payment to the domain's resolved wallet address does not necessarily reach or renew the domain contract.

## Expiration

Expiration does not automatically release the domain or remove its DNS records. After more than 366 days without renewal, anyone can start a new auction if none is active. Until that auction starts, the current owner can still renew.

An expired-domain auction removes the previous owner's control and records the opening bidder. It initially lasts one week; subsequent bids follow the same extension rules as new-domain auctions. The domain address and DNS records remain unchanged. Ownership is assigned through finalization after the auction ends.
