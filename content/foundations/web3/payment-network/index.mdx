TON Payment Network enables participants to exchange payments off-chain through interconnected payment channels. Smart contracts on TON secure the funds and provide mechanisms for on-chain settlement.

## Payment channels

A payment channel connects two participants. They exchange signed state updates to account for payments without submitting each payment as a separate blockchain transaction.

Funding and settlement involve on-chain operations. Participants can withdraw funds or close channels through the supported contract procedures. When the parties cannot cooperate, settlement follows the contract's dispute process and deadlines.

A node's wallet balance is separate from the funds available in its payment channels.

## Routed payments

Participants can pay through intermediate nodes instead of opening a direct channel with every recipient. Virtual channels connect the participants across a route of existing channels.

A route needs sufficient available capacity for the selected asset. Intermediaries can charge fees for providing this capacity and forwarding payments.

## Assets and fees

The implementation supports the native currency, jettons, and extra-currencies. Each node configures the assets it accepts and their associated fees and limits. Support for an asset does not guarantee that a suitable funded route is available.

Off-chain payments avoid a blockchain transaction for each payment, but are not necessarily free. Node fees and on-chain transaction fees are separate costs.

## Applications

The implementation provides a standalone Go node, an HTTP API, and a WebAssembly client that can run in a browser.

[TON Tunnel](/foundations/web3/ton-tunnel) uses Payment Network to pay relays for traffic. Channel operations are covered in this section; relay pricing and configuration are covered in [Accept traffic payments](/foundations/web3/ton-tunnel/payments).

For monitoring ordinary blockchain deposits and withdrawals, see [Payment processing](/applications/payments/overview).
